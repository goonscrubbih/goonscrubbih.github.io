About

Solo hobbyist developer. I build small, self-contained apps and tools on the side and give away what's actually free to give away.

Mission & scope

Everything on this site is a side project, built and maintained by one person in spare time. That means: no roadmap promises, no support SLA, and things ship when they're ready rather than on a schedule. What's listed as "in development" is genuinely in progress, not a placeholder for something already finished.

Apps here are free to download and use. If one saves you time or money, a donation (see below) is welcome but never required — nothing on this site is paywalled or ad-gated.
