ThaBrad keeps beating the first-place team

In Week 8 the BEAGLES were 7–0 and ThaBrad beat them. In Week 9 Buttons was 7–1 and first in points, and ThaBrad beat him by 72. C.J. Stroud scored 35.36, CeeDee Lamb 33.5, Tee Higgins 20.2, and he left 1.60 on the bench. Sex Panthers have won four straight and are 6–3.

Tbunzz saw it Sunday night: "B-rad on a heater," then "Knocking off the top team b2b weeks." ThaBrad, at 2:56 a.m.: "Oh yeah, takin down the big dawgs back to back. Feeling PRETTY good :)". Buttons still leads the series 7–3, but ThaBrad has won the last two.

Buttons filed his own report at 6:31 Sunday: "Oof dudding vs Sex." His 96.74 was his lowest of the year and his first under 100. Josh Jacobs scored 0.5, D'Andre Swift 3.1. At quarterback he started Kirk Cousins (12.64), the player he claimed on waivers Thursday at 2 a.m. and then called a "Scam" because he said he didn't mean to. Bryce Young scored 20.34 on his bench. It didn't matter. His best lineup scored 108.

Four at the top

The BEAGLES had their worst week too, 107.26, a hair under last week's 107.96. Josh Allen scored 12.86. Deion had Joe Burrow (29.54), Bijan Robinson (26.7) and Javonte Williams (22.4), and a kicker he added Saturday afternoon, Dominic Zvada, who scored 17. Deion won by 49.58. Zeus still leads their series 9–6.

Four teams are now 7–2: TYJT, Buttons, the BEAGLES and Stay Questionable, in that order on points. Since 2016, eight teams have been 7–2 after nine weeks and all eight made the playoffs. Sex Panthers and the Taylords are a game back at 6–3. ThaBrad: "Race tightening up at the top after this week." Two weeks ago Zeus was alone on the mountain. Now it's crowded up there, and he's third.

The Egbuka trade keeps getting worse for him. Jonah Coleman has scored 20.4 and 18.8 in two starts for Peeka. Emeka Egbuka was Out the first week and scored 6.9 this week.

TYJT scored 173.98 and needed most of it

traffikhan had the top score of the week and the fourth-best of the season: Christian Watson 30.1, Bucky Irving 28.5, Brock Purdy 25.28, Brock Bowers 22.3. Jarbelo saw it coming on Wednesday: "[traffikhan] is scheduled to wax me according to projections."

Tampa didn't get waxed. Matthew Golden scored 32.4, and Fairbairn, the kicker Jarbelo won on waivers last week, scored 17. Tampa's 156.50 beat eight of the other eleven teams and lost by 17.48. Tbunzz, late Sunday: "Damn J might lose with second highest score of the week." It ended up fourth. Jarbelo leads this series 6–2, and the last time they played he set the league's all-time scoring record against traffikhan, 198.78. He's 4–5 now. Six of the 25 teams that were 4–5 after nine weeks made the playoffs.

The desk was wrong again

Thursday's Matchup of the Week said Goudariddance would be 7–1 with his best lineups, pointed out that Lamar Jackson was ruled Out and still in his lineup, and listed the two swaps that would fix it: Matthew Stafford for Lamar and Devin Singletary for Samaje Perine. Goudariddance made both swaps, and then the desk picked Peeka anyway. Pukachu won by 22.64. He left 4.70 on the bench, his best week of lineup-setting all year. That's two wrong picks in a row.

Puka Nacua scored 35.0. The good news ends there. Tbunzz on Monday: "Puka to IR too." ThaBrad: "Puka in the game with 2 min left was crazy." Pukachu is 4–5 and the team is named after a player on injured reserve.

Schoolboi started his fifth quarterback of the year, Drew Lock, and was right again (12.6 to Geno Smith's 7.4). He left Courtland Sutton's 15.5 on the bench, but his best lineup scored 125.40 and still loses. Peeka is 2–7. Ten teams have been 2–7 after nine weeks, and none made the playoffs. The series is 2–1 Goudariddance.

Around the league

The Taylords have won six straight since 0–3. ThaBrad noticed at 1:10 this morning: "Just noticed [Tbunzz] started 0-3 and has won the last 6 games." Kenneth Walker scored 36.3, the most by any starter this week. Tbunzz also left Jordan Love's 36.18 on the bench behind Patrick Mahomes's 16.48, and Ladd McConkey, the receiver he traded for two minutes before kickoff last week, scored 0.0. He left 35.50 on the bench and won by 49.52. He leads this series 6–3.

JBV scored 71.46. That's the lowest score in the league this year, 11.74 below anyone else's worst. He picked up Kendre Miller on Thursday, started him, and got 0.0. Tucker Kraft scored 1.9. For the second week in a row Tampa Bay's defense outscored Denver's from his bench, 12 to 8. He is 0–9. From 2016 to 2025 no team in this league was 0–9 after nine weeks. The four worst were 1–8. The worst final record anyone has posted is 2–12.

Rage won again, and this one was not 182. Malik Willis, last week's waiver claim, scored 3.82. Ja'Marr Chase scored 31.5 and the Jacksonville defense 12. Rage's 115.22 was the eighth-best score of the week. It was enough because Iceman Williams scored 83.20, with A.J. Brown, Deebo Samuel and Garrett Wilson all scoring zero on his bench. Jov has lost five straight and is 2–7. Last Wednesday Goon posted: "Im counting the chuba deal as a win so im technically 2-7." He's actually 2–7 now. The trade tally this week: Chuba Hubbard 13.6, Garrett Wilson 0.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Nov 10 after Week 9 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. "vs. league" is each team's record if it had played every other team every week. Series records and playoff history cover ESPN (2016–2021) and Sleeper (2022–2026).

Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets. Transactions are from the league's own log. This league uses rolling waivers, not FAAB, so there are no bids to report.
