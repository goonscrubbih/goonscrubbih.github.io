The worst team in the league had the best week

Rage entered Week 8 at 0–7, with the fewest points in the league and a season high of 134.78. It scored 182.50. That beat all eleven other teams, and only two scores all season have been higher.

Goon added Tyler Allgeier on Saturday afternoon and dropped Tyjae Spears. On Sunday Allgeier ran 11 times for 147 yards, caught five passes and scored twice: 36.1, the third-best start in the league. He plays behind Jeremiyah Love in Arizona. Love scored 2.3 for Tampa. Tbunzz, Tuesday: "The Allgeier scoop n start was wild lol."

Ja'Marr Chase added 31.1 in the Bengals' 43–36 win, Chuba Hubbard 24.9 and the Jacksonville defense 21. Drake London scored 1.8 in the Tampa rain, and the team still cleared 180. With Malik Washington (15.0) in for London it would have been 195.70, just short of what Buttons calls the wall before 200. Jarbelo: "Putting up 182 this week after starting 0–7 is crazy."

It's also about seven weeks late. Last Wednesday Goon posted the league's history as a lifeline: "The worst start that still made the playoffs is 2–5." Rage started 0–7. The best Rage can finish is 7–7. Zeus told him "[Goon] ur dust" in September, and the standings still agree. Next week Rage plays Iceman Williams. Jov sent Hubbard to Rage in October and said "I got fleeced!!" that same night. Hubbard outscored Garrett Wilson by 10.6 this week.

Tee Higgins scored 37.5. Zeus: "Tee Higgins using my ass as a personal spitoon." ThaBrad, who said "It's my year baby!" last week, is 5–3. Tuesday morning: "Mmmm nice to wake up to W's."

Hall and Tate did sit, and Jarbelo got 29.9 from You're Darn Tuten, 26.8 from Michael Wilson ("garbage time goat") and 18.6 from Luther Burden, the receiver traffikhan has been trying to get for Juwan Johnson since September.

Joe Burrow's 39.48 was the best start of the week, and the other nine starters scored 65.4. On Tuesday Deion cut Kendrick Bourne, Tyler Loop and Mark Andrews, all in about a minute. He's 6–2, so he can afford it.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Nov 3 after Week 8 closed. NFL box-score lines are from ESPN. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. Defense points use this league's scoring settings.

Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets. Transactions are from the league's own log. This league uses rolling waivers, not FAAB, so there are no bids to report.
