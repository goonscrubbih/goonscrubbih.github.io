Same Bet, Other Side Of The World

The NFL plays its first regular-season game in Australia tonight, and the team named for Puka Nacua is stacking the Rams exactly the way JohnnyBoVonny stacked the Patriots on Wednesday. Johnny's 16.8 is the number to beat.

Sept 10, 2026 SF @ LAR · 8:35 PM ET · Melbourne Cricket Ground

The second game of the season is being played on a cricket ground in Melbourne at 10:35 on a Friday morning, local time. It is the NFL's first regular-season game in Australia: the Rams are the designated home team, and the 49ers are a week removed from a fifteen-hour chartered flight.

Eight of this league's 120 starters are in it. Nobody's injury tag survived the week — George Kittle's Achilles, Mike Evans's foot and Puka Nacua's undisclosed designation were all cleared by kickoff. Everyone who was supposed to play is playing.

Pukachu makes the Wednesday bet again

I Choose You Pukachu! — Goudariddance, owner of the draft's only A+ — starts Puka Nacua and the Rams defense in the same game. It is the identical shape of bet JohnnyBoVonny made on Wednesday with Drake Maye and the New England defense: one team, both sides of the ball. A comfortable Rams win pays both. A 49ers night sinks both together.

The coincidence is that they are playing each other. JohnnyBoVonny's version already landed — 16.8 points out of a 13–10 loss, Maye's 9.8 and the Patriots defense's 7.0. Pukachu has zero, and its Rams stack is the first chance to answer. Everything else on both rosters waits for Sunday.

The hedge sits on the bench: Matthew Stafford, the man throwing Puka the ball, is Pukachu's backup quarterback behind Lamar Jackson.

Wednesday's stack scored 16.8 in a loss. Tonight's has to beat it from zero.

The only head-to-head

One matchup has starters on both sides of this game: DeionSandalz vs Nacua Matata.

Nacua Matata leads 12.6–0 off Wednesday, most of it from a kicker, after A.J. Brown left in the third quarter with a high-ankle sprain. Tonight it adds two Rams — Kyren Williams at running back and Davante Adams at flex — on the same offense Pukachu is betting on.

DeionSandalz answers with one player: Mike Evans, in his first regular-season game as a 49er. If the Rams offense goes, Nacua Matata's lead grows twice over. If the 49ers take it, Deion closes the gap from one side while the other empties.
