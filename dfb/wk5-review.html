Buttons hit the wall

Sunday night Tbunzz did the math in the chat: "ur 1.5 points shy of the record." Buttons answered with the line of the week: "There's like a wall before 200 in this league." He finished at 197.36. In five seasons on Sleeper nobody has cleared 200, and the closest anyone has come is 198.78.

That 198.78 is Jarbelo's, from Week 8 last season. This week Jarbelo scored 83.72.

Jahmyr Gibbs scored 37.7, Malik Nabers 29.1 and George Pickens 28.0. Isaiah Likely put up 32.4 at flex, and Buttons called it at 4 p.m.: "Likely to PoP! Poppin." Kyler Murray, picked up Sunday morning for Deshaun Watson, scored 18.3. Buttons beat all eleven teams this week and is 5–0.

Which means Fraud Watch has a problem. A week ago Buttons was 22–22 against the league and seventh in points. Now he is 33–22 and third. The desk is moving the badge. See the awards.

Perfect lineup, disputed receiver

The Taylords left zero points on the bench, the only perfect lineup of the week. Parker Washington, the most argued-about player in the league, scored 30.4. The desk's board had him WR41 in August. Tbunzz has defended him all season, and this week he has a receipt.

The lineup was perfect by a rounding error. Jacoby Brissett started and scored 25.28. Jordan Love sat on the bench and scored exactly 25.28. The champion is 2–3 and climbing, and Jov is 2–3 and asking the chat for a running back. Alvin Kamara scored 2.6, and Saquon Barkley sat with a zero.

Jov also cut Kyle Pitts early Sunday morning, six days after adding him to laugh at Jarbelo. Pitts never started a game for Chubba. Kendrick Bourne, his replacement, scored 12.0 on the bench. Jov's eight-leg parlay was down to one leg, the Ravens, with a 1,000 percent cash-out on the table. Goon asked Monday night whether he took it. No answer yet.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Oct 13 after Week 5 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. "Second-highest score" and "second-biggest margin" rank every regular-season and playoff game this league has played on Sleeper, 2022 to now; the record is 198.78 (Jarbelo, Week 8, 2025) and the season's biggest margin is Sex Panthers over Rage in Week 4, by 90.8. Defense points use this league's scoring settings.

Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed. Transactions are from the league's own log. This league uses rolling waivers, not FAAB, so there are no bids to report.
