K&Y Matchup of the Week · Week 4

Tampa Wins in September. Jov Wins When It Counts.

Tampa Tropics and Saquan Deebos Chubba are 0.01 points apart as the lineups sit. Jov has never beaten Tampa before Week 10. He has also beaten them in a semifinal, by 42, with the best score of his life.

Oct 1, 2026Tampa Tropics (3–0) vs Saquan Deebos Chubba (1–2)Week 4 preview

The brief this week was new blood: no team that has already had the K&Y. That left two games, and only one of them has a body buried in it.

Tampa's manager is the commissioner, the loudest man in the chat after Zeus, and the owner of the best regular-season record in the league two years running. Jov has posted 16 messages all year. One of them was "I put in PTO for this." In December 2025, Jov ended Tampa's season.

Every meeting since 2022, playoffs included. Jov's two highest scores ever, 187.60 and 186.16, both came against Tampa.

Read the dates. Tampa's wins came in Weeks 2, 2 and 11. Jov's came in Weeks 10, 13, 13 and the semifinal. Tampa is 2–0 against Jov in September and 1–4 the rest of the time. It is the entire Tampa franchise in one table.

Because here is what happens to Tampa once the calendar turns. In 2024 they went 11–3, lost the semifinal to TYJT, then lost the third-place game to The Taylords. In 2025 they went 12–2, ran into Jov's 187.60 in the semifinal, then lost the third-place game to Pukachu. Best record in the league twice. Fourth place twice. Not one ring in four years, while holding the highest single score of the Sleeper era (198.78, 2025 Week 8).

Third place exists in this league because Jarbelo argued on draft day that it "is fun to add another game to the championship week." He has lost that game two years running. Fun for whom, exactly.

So Tampa is 3–0 again, with the second-most points in the league, playing the man who knocked them out. In Week 4. Which is September for one more day, if you squint.

Tampa Tropics: JSN and the commissioner's bench

Jaxon Smith-Njigba has scored 26.20, 42.50 and 35.36: 104 points in three weeks, nearly a quarter of everything Tampa has scored. Jarbelo's stated plan, from Week 1: "I just need 60 from jj and jsn a week and im good." The two of them have put up 57.4, 51.0 and 40.6. Trending the wrong way, and Tampa is 3–0 anyway.

The "jj" half of that plan is Justin Jefferson, now questionable and sitting on Tampa's bench at 16.4 projected. Starting in his place, at RB2: Keaton Mitchell, also questionable, projected 4.7, after scoring 0.90 and 4.90. Jarbelo dropped Mitchell in Week 3 and picked him back up on Wednesday. If the Jefferson-for-Mitchell swap happens before Sunday, Tampa gains 11.7 points and this preview is a waste of everyone's time.

The rest is a roster in constant motion. Breece Hall, one of the five "Could-Bees" Zeus named on draft night, is doubtful. The tight end slot holds Kenyon Sadiq, questionable, with Dalton Schultz in a flex. Tampa has made 18 roster moves, the most in the league, including dropping Kyle Pitts on Tuesday. Sex Panthers have made one.

"This is what happens when I drink and draft."

Jarbelo, the night he took Kyle Pitts. Dropped him 24 days later.

The Pitts arc deserves its own paragraph. Drafted on draft night, explained the same evening. Too crossfaded at that point in the draft, per TYJT, and Jarbelo agreed: "Yeah basically." By Week 3 he was "having nightmares of drafting Kyle pitts." Zeus called it the Pitts of Despair. Tuesday, he was cut. Buttons warned him he was "about to pop." Jarbelo: "Probably now that I dropped him."

And as Rage can confirm, Tampa is 5–0 against Rage all-time, which the commissioner summarised in the chat as "[Goon]'s my bitch." The desk notes it. The desk is not over it.

Draft day, Jov said: "Can't wait to steal Jalen hurts from [Zeus] again." Hurts went to Sex Panthers. Some plans don't survive the first round.

The call

As set at 1 a.m. Thursday, this is 120.79 to 120.80, the closest projection the desk has ever printed. It will not stay there. Jefferson goes in or he doesn't, McConkey plays or he doesn't, and Tampa's commissioner will make at least two more roster moves before Sunday, because he always does.

The desk picks Tampa. JSN has a 26-point floor so far, Jefferson should replace a 4.7, and history says Tampa doesn't lose to Jov in the first half of the season. History also says that doesn't matter in the slightest, and Jarbelo knows exactly when it starts mattering.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 4 PPR projections summed over each team's starting lineup as set at 1 a.m. ET Thursday, Oct 1. Injury tags are Sleeper's at the same time. The series includes every regular-season and playoff meeting in this league on Sleeper, 2022–2025, plus 2026. Third-place games are from Sleeper's playoff bracket. Quotes are from the league chat, verbatim; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets. The pick was made on merit among games featuring two teams that hadn't had a K&Y this season: this one and Stay Questionable vs Peeka Egbuka.
