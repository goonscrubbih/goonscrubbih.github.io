Opening night · Week 1

Most Of You Are Just Watching

The season opens tonight with New England at Seattle, and eight of this league’s 120 starters are in it. The Matchup of the Week has nobody at all.

Sep 9, 2026 Week 1 · NE @ SEA, 8:20 PM ET

The 2026 season starts in half an hour and eight of this league's 120 starters are in the game. New England at Seattle, 8:20 Eastern, and everybody else waits until Sunday.

Four teams have nobody in it at all. One of them is the reigning champion. Another is the team that just got the Matchup of the Week billing — the Taylords and Benjamin's Buttons, the last two men to hold the trophy, and between them they have zero players on the field tonight. The marquee game opens with both benches watching the same broadcast as everyone else.

Which leaves the season's first four hours to a handful of managers, and two of them made a bet worth looking at.

One team bet New England twice

JohnnyBoVonny has the most riding on tonight and the least protection. He starts Drake Maye at quarterback — the third-best QB anybody rostered and his single biggest positional edge — and he starts the New England defense behind him.

That is the same bet twice. If the Patriots move the ball, Maye pays and the defense probably does not. If Seattle buries them, both scores die together. He has bought correlation in a format where correlation is what turns a bad Sunday into an unrecoverable one, and he has done it in the one game where he cannot see how the rest of the week is going first.

It gets slightly worse. His best remaining stash, Rashid Shaheed, is a Seahawk sitting on his bench — the hedge he owns and is not using.

Two teams accidentally hedged. One deliberately doubled down.

The line that moved

One number changed more than any other since draft night. Tampa Tropics was favoured over Rage by seven points on Saturday. Tonight Rage is favoured by 87.

Nothing dramatic caused it. Rage filled the empty defense slot the draft recap flagged, moved MarShawn Lloyd into the lineup when TreVeyon Henderson was ruled out with an ankle, and added a receiver. Tampa stood still. Ninety-four points of swing from housekeeping.

It matters because of the history. Tampa Tropics is 4–0 all-time against Rage and the margin has collapsed every year: 32 points, then 58, then 17, then 1.5 last season. Rage has been getting closer for four years and has never broken through. This is the first time the projection has ever pointed the other way.

Tampa gets the head start regardless. Smith-Njigba plays tonight; Rage has nobody until Sunday, and spends three days watching a lead it cannot answer.

What is not happening tonight

Ja'Marr Chase is fully cleared — the knee that carried a questionable tag all week has no designation at all. He plays Sunday.

TreVeyon Henderson is out with an ankle and sits on injured reserve. He is a Patriot, so tonight is the game he would have played.

And Nacua Matata enters Week 1 with no defense on the roster, having dropped the Bears to stash Isiah Pacheco off IR. That is a mandatory starting slot scoring zero, in a matchup already projected 254 points the wrong way.

How these numbers were produced

Rosters, starting lineups and injury designations pulled live from Sleeper at 7:45 PM ET, about half an hour before kickoff. Lineups can change until each player's game starts, so a name here may not be a name that plays.

The projections are the same season-long value model used for the draft review — a decay curve fitted to positional rank, not real weekly projections. They rank rosters; they do not predict scores. Treat a 22-point gap as a coin flip and a 254-point gap as a mismatch, and do not read the decimals.
