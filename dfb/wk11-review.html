For the third week running, no loser's best lineup would have won. The closest was JBV, whose best was 115.46 against Rage's 117.58.

The trade that happened, and the one that didn't

Friday at noon a trade went pending between the BEAGLES and JBV. Eighteen minutes later it was cancelled. The commissioner's rule is that a team out of the playoffs can't trade, and JBV was 0–10. Jarbelo called it "always been the rule." Zeus called it "Heinous acts by commish imo," and pointed out that the Taylords' three-way deal with a winless JBV had gone through in October. Jarbelo's answer: JBV was 0–6 then and "Could have won out and gone 8–6 just saying." JBV himself asked for it to go through: "I can't go out with no wins man. It's for my confidence."

Zeus left with a promise: "I'll be doing this trade or getting Ja'Marr Chase from [Goon] by the end of the day." Jarbelo: "If [Zeus] can get Chase I can't veto for sure." Rage was 3–7, not yet mathematically out.

Sunday at 12:54, six minutes before kickoff, Chase went to the BEAGLES for Jaylen Warren and Jalen Coker. The chat did not approve. Deion: "Coker borderline droppable." Buttons wondered what Zeus had said to get it done "when this one was rejected." Deion had the line of the week: "[Zeus] just used every single bit of KY jelly he's won from the matchups over the years in one night on [Goon]."

Then the games were played. Chase scored 8.1. Warren and Coker scored 13.6 between them, and ThaBrad's review was "And Coker died." The desk's own math, which Deion called "dumb" when it got pasted into the chat, had the whole deal about even and Coker-for-Chase costing about 7 a week. Week one: Coker 8.7, Chase 8.1. Chase has averaged 21.5 this season, so nobody should get comfortable.

The Taylords by 62

Zeus came in 4–0 in K&Y games, and he'd just landed the best receiver in the league. He scored 92.36, the second-lowest score of the week, and beat one team on the whole board. Josh Allen had 12.16. Tyler Warren had 3.2 on Thursday night, and Zeus told him the next morning, "you been a real piece of shit lately." Khalil Shakir scored 15.3 on the bench.

The Taylords had Mahomes at 24.96, Emmett Johnson at 24.3 and the Bears defense at 16. Tbunzz's Tuesday update: "8 in a row for the Taylords." Jarbelo, underneath it: "I'm 1–7 in that time." The series that started with Peyton's seven touchdowns is now tied 8–8. Tbunzz has won the last three, by 52, 97 and 62. Tbunzz also remembered the 2017 semifinal Zeus won by 2.14: "that was the Julio drop game vs the Bucs that we went to."

The desk was right, twice

The desk picked the Taylords in the K&Y Matchup and Rage in the undercard. Both won. It was the desk's first pick against the BEAGLES in a K&Y game, and it landed by 62.

Around the league

ThaBrad came into the year with the worst record of the Sleeper era, 20–36, and now holds the second seed. He scored 155.36 with a perfect lineup, beat all eleven teams, and has won six straight. His best Sleeper season was 6–8. His best ever were 8–5 in 2018 and 2019, so one more win sets a career high. Buttons, Tuesday: "Wow sex jumped to 2nd." ThaBrad: "That's right baby."

Jonah Coleman scored 34.9 for Peeka Egbuka. In September, Zeus listed Coleman among his "league winners — waiver wire pickups drafted by me!" He's winning leagues for Schoolboi now.

JohnnyBoVonny is 0–11. His last three: Pukachu, Jov, and then Jarbelo in Week 14. If he gets there winless, Jarbelo gets the last shot at sealing 0–14. The simulations put it at 26%, up from 17% a week ago.

Pukachu won by 61.68 and is the only team below .500 with a real pulse: 5–6, two games out with three to play, about 2%.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Nov 25 after Week 11 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. "vs. league" is each team's record if it had played every other team every week. Trades and cancelled trades come from the league's transaction log and chat.

Playoff and bye odds come from 100,000 simulations of Weeks 12–14 on the real schedule. Each team's weekly score is drawn from a normal distribution with its 2026 mean and spread. Six teams make it, the top two get byes, and ties go to points. Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed, with nicknames in brackets.
