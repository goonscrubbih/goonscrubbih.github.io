K&Y Matchup of the Week · Week 9

Pukachu Could Be 7–1

Goudariddance has left more points on his bench than anyone in the league. Schoolboi has set two perfect lineups and lost both. Between them they have posted 19 messages in the group chat all year.

Nov 5, 2026I Choose You Pukachu! (3–5) vs Peeka Egbuka (2–6)Week 9 preview

This is the quiet game. Zeus has posted 831 messages in the league chat this year. Goudariddance has posted eight, and the last one was on Sept 7, an invite to watch the Week 1 games at his place. Schoolboi has posted 11. Neither of them has said a word about this matchup, so the numbers will have to do the talking.

The numbers have plenty to say. Both teams are named after a receiver. Goudariddance still has his: Puka Nacua is on the roster, nicknamed "Pukachu." Schoolboi traded his namesake to Zeus last week. And both of them have been beaten in ways that should not be possible.

The backstory

Schoolboi joined in 2024, so they have only met twice, and the series is 1–1. Both games were blowouts, one each way.

Their seasons have gone the same way. Goudariddance was going to host the draft party at his new house, with burgers. It got cancelled the morning of the draft. Schoolboi drew the last pick and called it before anyone else could: "last pick?! Who rigged this," and then, "I'll make last pick work." Two months later, both of them are near the bottom of the standings, and neither one has complained about it in the chat.

They do have one thing in common with last year. In 2025 Goudariddance was 3–5 after eight weeks, the same as now. He won five of his last six, finished 8–6 and took third place. When Goon posted a list of the worst starts that still made the playoffs, Goudariddance 2025 was on it.

Pukachu: the bench is the opponent

Goudariddance is the unluckiest manager in league history: a career 26–30 record on a .568 all-play, the biggest gap in the league. This year the schedule is not the problem. He's 37–51 against everyone, which says 3–5 is about right. The problem is the lineup. He has started 84.0% of his possible points, the lowest rate in the league, and left 185.8 points on the bench.

Run his best possible lineup against what each opponent actually scored, and four of his five losses turn into wins. Week 2 against Tampa, by 0.12. Week 4 against Buttons, the 0.08 loss on Bryce Young's kneel-downs. Week 6 against Deion. Week 8 against the Taylords, by 23. With perfect lineups Goudariddance would be 7–1, tied with Zeus and Buttons for the best record in the league.

Week 8 needs a footnote. He lost to the Taylords by 3.02, and Lamar Jackson scored 0.0. That one wasn't a lineup choice. Lamar got hurt early on Sunday ("Lamar out. Brutal," ThaBrad posted at 1:40), and Matthew Stafford's 8.28 sat on the bench. That's the bad luck again.

"[Goudariddance] and [Tbunzz]'s shitty luck strikes again."

Buttons, Sept 30. Tbunzz at least has a ring. Goudariddance is still waiting.

This week, the footnote is the lineup. As of Thursday afternoon, Lamar is still at quarterback, and he's ruled Out with a thumb injury. Baltimore is starting Jacoby Brissett tonight. Stafford (18.6) is on the bench. Swap him in and Pukachu projects 128.08. Move Devin Singletary (8.7) in for Samaje Perine (6.5) too and it's 130.33, within three points of Peeka. Goudariddance has until 8:15 tonight to move Lamar. After kickoff Lamar's slot locks, and Pukachu plays with no quarterback.

Peeka Egbuka: perfect, and 2–6

Schoolboi has scored 984.26 points this year, more than Pukachu. He's 2–6 anyway, because his opponents have scored 1,147.92 against him, the most in the league. His 41–47 all-play is seventh-best and says he should be closer to 4–4.

He sets lineups as well as anyone. Peeka has started 93% of its possible points, third in the league behind Jarbelo and Deion. Twice this year Schoolboi started his best possible lineup. In Week 6 he lost to the Taylords by 1.32. In Week 8 he scored 135.90 and ran into Rage's 182.50, the top score of the week. Only once in eight weeks could any lineup on his roster have won.

The roster has changed a lot. His starting quarterbacks so far: Jaxson Dart, Geno Smith, Sam Darnold, Aaron Rodgers and Geno again. On Oct 7 he got Jaxon Smith-Njigba from Tampa for Michael Wilson and Bhayshul Tuten, and JSN (24.0) is the top projected player in this game. Then Zeus spent two days chasing him ("@SchoolboiO respond to me!!!!") until Schoolboi sent Emeka Egbuka to the BEAGLES for Jonah Coleman. Zeus celebrated, then found out Egbuka was hurt: "Got fleeced Egbuka out!" Coleman starts in a flex this week at 14.4.

"[Schoolboi] got to change his team name / He traded his namesake"

Jarbelo, Nov 3. As of Thursday the team is still called Peeka Egbuka.

Where it gets decided

Tonight, Jacksonville at Baltimore. Derrick Henry and Cam Little play for Schoolboi. Lamar plays for nobody. If Goudariddance hasn't moved him by 8:15, Peeka starts with a 14.6-point head start at quarterback before anyone in Pukachu's lineup plays.

Sunday morning, Cincinnati vs Atlanta. The two Bengals backs, one on each side. Schoolboi starts Chase Brown (16.2), Goudariddance starts Samaje Perine (6.5).

Sunday at 4:25, Arizona at Seattle. Trey McBride for Pukachu, JSN for Peeka, in the same game. Puka goes earlier, Rams at Washington with the Rams defense, against Stefon Diggs. The last starter on either side is Colston Loveland, Tampa Bay at Chicago on Sunday night.

The stakes

From 2016 to 2025, 25 teams were 4–5 after nine weeks and six of them made the playoffs. Goudariddance was one of them last year. Sixteen were 3–6 and only two made it. Ten teams were 2–7, and none of them made it. If Goudariddance wins he stays alive. If Schoolboi wins, he becomes one of the 3–6 teams instead of one of the 2–7 ones.

The call

As set, Sleeper has Peeka by 23.79. With the obvious swap it's 5.19, and with Goudariddance's best lineup it's 2.94.

The desk picks Peeka Egbuka. Schoolboi's lineup has no injury tags and the best player in the game, and he plays first tonight. Pukachu has two questionable starters and a quarterback who isn't playing. Schoolboi's opponents have averaged 143.49 against him this year. This week his opponent might not have a quarterback. The desk got last week wrong; its streak ended at three.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 9 PPR projections, summed over each starting lineup as set at 2:14 p.m. ET Thursday, Nov 5. Injury tags and bye weeks are Sleeper's at the same time. "Best possible lineup" is the highest-scoring legal lineup from each roster using actual points, compared against what the opponent actually scored; lineup rate is points scored divided by Sleeper's maximum possible points. The series covers every Sleeper meeting since Schoolboi joined in 2024. Playoff odds count every team's record after Weeks 8 and 9 of each regular season from 2016 to 2025 (ESPN and Sleeper) and whether it reached the six-team playoff bracket. Chat counts cover every message in the league chat this year. Quotes are from the league chat, verbatim; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets.
