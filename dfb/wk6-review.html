The mountain is full

Two weeks ago Zeus announced that he and Buttons "stand on the undefeated mountain." Both are still up there. Monday night, with McCaffrey still running, Zeus did the scheduling math: "Two 6-0 teams face each other next week…"

Buttons got there with the top score of the week, 138.84, which beat all eleven teams. He did it without Jahmyr Gibbs, who was on bye. Two weeks ago Buttons accused Jarbelo of using "his commish powers" to schedule him on Gibbs's bye week. The commissioner got the matchup he supposedly arranged and lost by 25. Malik Nabers scored 28.5, Isaiah Likely 23.9 and D'Andre Swift 23.0. The only blemish was Terry McLaurin, now listed out, who scored 3.7. Buttons, Tuesday: "Terry Dust… [Jarbelo] was right." Jarbelo: "If anyone knows about dusty players it's me."

The BEAGLES beat Iceman Williams by 19. On Sunday night Zeus asked for "a cool 12 from McCaff tomm night." McCaffrey gave him 30.7, the best start in the league, from the running back Jarbelo said in Week 1 might be "a rb2 at #5." Zeus had already taken his victory lap last Wednesday: "Might've drafted an RB2 in the first but drafted a RB1 in multiple other rounds muhahahahaah."

Zeus also called "TOP SCORE for this week" during the Monday game. He finished third, four hundredths behind the Taylords. A week after "Eagles might be cooked," DeVonta Smith and Dallas Goedert came back for 32.6 between them. Then on Monday morning Jalen Hurts was ruled out for the season. Zeus, Eagles fan: "What the absolute fuck."

Next week Josh Allen is on bye. Zeus picked up Kirk Cousins on Wednesday, nicknamed him "Kirko Chainz," and told the chat he's "so ready to douse [Buttons]'s ass in KY even though Josh Allen on a bye!" Buttons: "I'll be watching Gibbs fresh off bye." One of them comes down Sunday.

Rage, and the quarterback on waivers

On Saturday the desk told Goon to pick up Kirk Cousins, projected 17.7, and drop Jameis Winston. Rage started Winston. Winston scored 6.48. Cousins scored 19.52 for nobody. Rage lost to TYJT by 10.08. With Cousins, Rage wins by about three. Bo Nix had already scored 4.48 on the bench on Thursday.

Four days later, Zeus picked up Cousins. Rage is 0–6 and 17–49 against the league. Goon spent the week teasing championship rings and a "prototype trophy" in the chat. Next up: the Taylords.

TYJT won without trying very hard. traffikhan started Brock Purdy, who scored 17.36. Trevor Lawrence scored 27.34 on his bench. He is 5–1 and first in points anyway, and his Rays are going to the World Series. "Rays going to the World Series boys!" So, loudly, was Zeus, the man traffikhan once caught being "not a Tampa fan": "YANDY!!!!!!!!"

Perfect, and beaten

Peeka Egbuka left 0.00 points on the bench. Every slot held the best available player. Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 25.4, Colston Loveland 18.5 and Emeka Egbuka 17.8. Peeka scored 131.12, fifth in the league, and lost by 1.32.

The Taylords won with 20.5 on their bench, including 25.7 from Germie Bernard. Kenneth Walker scored 27.9, the third-best start of the week. In September Jarbelo said he "would never think the person who drafted Ken Walker would be 0-3." The defending champ is 3–3 now.

When the chat argued about who has the dustiest roster, Schoolboi, who almost never posts, spoke up: "nah it's def my team." Tbunzz: "[JBV]'s roster might have something to say about that lol."

The Matchup of the Week: two in a row

The desk picked Stay Questionable. Deion won by 23. Tyler Shough scored 26.96 and DK Metcalf 20.3. Deion still started Quinshon Judkins, who was out, for zero: "Fuk man judkins out an didn't see." The desk's Matchup picks are now right two weeks running, after missing on Tampa in Week 4.

Goudariddance's best lineup would have won. Tre Tucker scored 26.7 on his bench. That's the same Tre Tucker Jarbelo dropped back in September, prompting Buttons to say Jarbelo "already dropped a number 1 wr." Goud left 24.2 on the bench and lost by 23.08. Pukachu is the only loser this week whose roster had a winning lineup in it.

A correction. On Thursday Deion told the chat the desk had been "conflating our chats." He says "we making the playoffs" was about the Bucs, not his own team, and that some of what the desk quoted came from his other league. The desk accepts that. The line is retired. Deion is 5–1 anyway.

Around the league

Sex Panthers beat JBV by 44.58, the week's biggest margin. C.J. Stroud scored 22.98 and James Cook 24.3. ThaBrad is 3–3, and Jalen Hurts, ruled out for the season on Monday, was on his bench.

JohnnyBoVonny scored 87.30, the week's lowest, and lost to all eleven teams. He started Cam Skattebo and Jacory Croskey-Merritt, now on IR. They combined for zero. On Saturday he put Jayden Daniels "on sale" because "I need to start someone." Daniels scored 21.0 on the bench. JBV, overnight Tuesday: "Dawg. It's bad." Jarbelo, Wednesday: "[JBV] called me yesterday and we talked about how bad our teams are." The brothers are 3–3 and 0–6.

Tampa Tropics got 24.7 from Bhayshul Tuten, the "You're Darn Tuten" pickup. TYJT, live: "YOU'RE DARN FRICKEN TUTEN!!" Jarbelo: "Finally." Then: "Teams in shambles lol. Tuten save me!" Justin Jefferson, Jeremiyah Love, Breece Hall, Zach Charbonnet and T.J. Hockenson all sat on his bench at zero. Tampa is 3–3.

Iceman Williams is Jov's new name for the team formerly known as Saquan Deebos Chubba. Saquon and Deebo, two of the three namesakes, sat on the bench. The third, Chuba Hubbard, plays for Rage now. Jov picked Kyle Pitts back up on Saturday, his second stint with the tight end Jarbelo cut, and Pitts scored 23.0. Jov spent Sunday morning offering "Deebo and Ladd package deal" for a running back. He is 2–4.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Oct 21 after Week 6 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. Kirk Cousins's 19.52 is his Week 6 score in this league's scoring; he was unrostered all week. The desk's Saturday advice to Rage is on file in the desk's notes. Defense points use this league's scoring settings.

Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets. Transactions are from the league's own log. This league uses rolling waivers, not FAAB, so there are no bids to report.
