The Commissioner Is Selling

Jarbelo started 3–0, lost four straight and put his whole team up for trade. Deion is 6–1 and says nobody's noticed. The projections have them 0.62 points apart.

Oct 29, 2026Tampa Tropics (3–4) vs Stay Questionable (6–1)Week 8 preview

Tuesday, a few minutes after the Week 7 recap went up, the commissioner posted: "Still looking to flip the whole team." Then: "Send trades." Then he pitched ThaBrad directly: "[ThaBrad] fleece me," "I can help you win," "Whatever you want man," and finally "Down to trade everyone."

The same minute, Deion posted about his own team: "Your boy sliding up that leader board under the radar." On Sunday the two of them play each other, and the projections can't pick a winner.

The backstory

They have played eleven times since 2016, and Jarbelo leads the series 7–4. Most of the games were lopsided: 177.04 to 111.52 in 2020, 167.20 to 118.08 for Deion in 2018. The closest was a 2022 consolation game in Week 16, 77.50 to 72.94, which neither of them should remember fondly. Last season Jarbelo won 174.50 to 125.60 on his way to a 12–2 record.

Then there's Matthew Golden. Deion had him in September and cut him on Sept 8, two days before the season opened, for Dylan Sampson. Tbunzz picked Golden up the next day. When Golden broke out, Deion posted on Sept 25: "I'm feeling GREAT about dropping Matthew golden before week 1 🤡." Jarbelo admitted he'd passed on him too: "I remember looking at golden on the waiver wire and thinking nah I already have reed lol."

On Oct 23 Jarbelo got Golden anyway, in the three-way deal with Tbunzz and JBV. Jarbelo sent out Justin Jefferson, T.J. Hockenson and Zonovan Knight, and got back Sam LaPorta and Golden. Golden starts for Tampa against the man who cut him, and he plays first: tonight at Green Bay, questionable with a shoulder injury. Sampson is on Deion's bench, also questionable.

Tampa Tropics: everything must go

Over the last two seasons Jarbelo has been the best regular-season manager in the league, 23–5 across 2024 and 2025. Both years he lost in the semifinal and then the third-place game. This year he started 3–0, scoring 144.90, 167.46 and 134.60. Then he lost four straight, scoring 101.90, 83.72, 113.42 and 89.78, and was last in the league twice. He's 29–48 against everyone, 4th-lowest in points, and has made 47 roster moves, more than anyone else in the league.

History says Week 8 matters for him. Of 34 teams that were 3–4 after seven weeks, 11 made the playoffs. Jarbelo is one of them: 3–4 in 2021, playoffs anyway. He was 3–4 in 2016 and 2022 too, and missed both times. Win Sunday and he's 4–4; half the teams at 4–4 made it (17 of 34). Lose and he's 3–5, and only 7 of 27 of those made it.

"I want to fire Bowles and get Jeremiah smith so I hope we stay bad"

Jarbelo, Oct 7, about the Buccaneers. He might not have meant the Tropics too.

The lineup he's selling still projects within a point of Deion's. Three of the "Could-Bees" Zeus named on draft night are still starting: Dak, Love and Breece. JSN went to Peeka on Oct 7, and Pitts was dropped in September. Breece Hall, Jeremiyah Love, Carnell Tate and Golden are all questionable, four of his ten starters. Michael Wilson (15.8, also questionable) is his best backup. Tampa's last player is Luther Burden, Chicago at Seattle on Monday night.

Stay Questionable: under the radar, as advertised

Deion is 6–1 with 949.1 points, fourth in the league, and 51–26 against everyone. His only loss was in Week 2, to traffikhan. He's won five straight since. He was right on Tuesday: Zeus and Buttons get the attention, and Deion keeps quietly winning.

This is new for him. In the four Sleeper seasons he went 27–29 and never finished better than 8–6. He has made the playoffs three times in ten years: 2017 and 2020 on ESPN, 2023 on Sleeper. Every team that was 6–1 after Week 7 made the playoffs, four of four. With a win he's 7–1, and all four of those made it too.

The team name is a joke, and the roster has earned it. On Oct 17 Deion asked the chat, "This has to be the worst year yet for injuries right?" Then: "Am I really about to have to start Brandin Cooks in 2026 👀." Then Mike Evans: "Mike with the surprise Achilles injury on the report. Hope it's not too serious." Evans is on IR. The next day: "Fuk man judkins out an didn't see." Deion cut Cooks on Tuesday. This week Chris Olave, Kaelon Black ("One of my guys this season") and his backup quarterback are all on bye.

And yet: the team called Stay Questionable has no questionable starters. Tampa has four. Deion's strength is his backfield: Bijan Robinson (21.0) and Javonte Williams (18.9) project 39.9 together, 14.3 more than Hall and Tuten. His weakness is receiver. Antonio Williams (7.9) starts because Evans and Olave can't, and Brenton Strange is in a flex as his second tight end.

Where it gets decided

Tonight, Carolina at Green Bay. Golden, if he plays. Nobody else in this matchup plays Thursday.

Sunday, Arizona at Dallas. The same offense, split between them. Dak Prescott (22.7) is Jarbelo's quarterback and Javonte Williams (18.9) is Deion's running back. Jeremiyah Love, Arizona's back, starts for Jarbelo too. If the Cowboys pass, Tampa gets the points. If they run, Deion does.

Sunday, Cleveland at Pittsburgh. Deion plays against himself. He starts DK Metcalf and the Steelers defense, and also Quinshon Judkins, Cleveland's running back. Every Judkins touchdown costs his own defense points.

The call

Sleeper has Deion by 0.62. That is a coin flip.

The desk picks Stay Questionable. Four of Tampa's starters are questionable, and Jarbelo has averaged 97.2 over the last four weeks, 29.7 below what this lineup projects. Deion has no injury tags in his lineup and the two best running backs in the game. If Golden plays well tonight against the man who cut him, Jarbelo might call off the sale. If not, expect more trade offers. The desk has picked right three weeks in a row.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 8 PPR projections, summed over each starting lineup as set at 12:35 p.m. ET Thursday, Oct 29. Injury tags and bye weeks are Sleeper's at the same time. Playoff odds count every team's record after Weeks 7 and 8 of each regular season from 2016 to 2025 and whether it reached the six-team playoff bracket. Roster-move counts are completed 2026 transactions, trades included. Quotes are from the league chat, verbatim; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets.
