The backstory

This series used to be one-sided. Zeus won the first nine meetings, from Week 5 of 2018 to Week 6 of 2024. Some were blowouts: 176.60 to 99.20 in 2018, 191.42 to 141.72 in 2022, and 133.60 to 67.04 that December. The last one was the closest game they've ever played. In Week 6 of 2024 Zeus won 107.96 to 107.56, by 0.40.

Every meeting between the two, ESPN (2018–2021) and Sleeper (2022–2025). All eleven were regular-season games. ThaBrad joined the league in 2018.

Then came 2025. ThaBrad won in Week 3 by 37, and won again in Week 14, the last week of the regular season, holding Zeus to 65.80. Those are the only two games ThaBrad has ever won in this series, and he won them both by more than Zeus beat him by in six of his nine wins.

The two managers have had very different careers. Zeus has two titles, 2017 on ESPN and 2022 on Sleeper, and was the runner-up in 2023. ThaBrad went 20–36 over the four Sleeper seasons, the worst record in the league, and hasn't made the playoffs since 2019. He is 4–3 now, the same number of wins he finished with in all of 2024.

The BEAGLES: unbeaten, and still asking for trades

Zeus is 7–0, with the most points in the league (1,004.1) and a 63–14 record against everyone. His smallest winning margin is 10.28, last week against Buttons. In the chat he still talks like a team that needs help. Tuesday: "Still looking for that 2 fa 1," "Got plenty RBs," "Make me shit deal brooooos." Wednesday night: "Send trades," then "Actually trying to make a smaller play!"

He does have plenty of RBs. Jaylen Warren, Ollie Gordon, Rhamondre Stevenson and Jonah Coleman are all starting. McCaffrey is on bye with a calf injury. Coleman is the one Zeus keeps bringing up. On Tuesday: "Jonah Coleman heating up!!! … Check the snap share if you dare muahahaha." At 7:04 this morning: "Jonah Coleman RB1 on Broncos depth chart 🗿" The projections still have Coleman at 8.8, the lowest of any running back or receiver in Zeus's lineup.

Zeus's two weak spots are both on the bench. DeVonta Smith (13.5) and Tyler Warren (11.3) both project higher than Coleman, but both are questionable, and so is DJ Moore, who starts. Moore and Josh Allen are both Bills now, so Zeus has 38.3 projected points in Buffalo against Baltimore. The other weak spot is his schedule. Seattle's defense against Chicago on Monday night is the last game in this matchup, and Zeus is the only one with a player in it.

Sex Panthers: the stars are there, the running backs aren't

ThaBrad is 4–3 and has won three of his last four. One of those was the 191.32 he put on Rage in Week 4, then the fourth-best score of the Sleeper era ("Can't believe I wasted two 40 bergers on rage"). Since then he's beaten JBV and Jarbelo by more than 40 each. He is 40–37 against everyone, a .519 team that's finally winning the games it should.

Zeus was right about the stars and the backfield. CeeDee Lamb (21.2), Tee Higgins (15.8) and Tetairoa McMillan (15.2) project 52.2 together. Zeus's two starting receivers project 29.4. Kittle, the third heavy hitter Zeus named, is on bye. Next to James Cook is Blake Corum at 9.5. ThaBrad described him Tuesday: "Corum is just one kyren shredded chicken from becoming CMC." Kyren Williams isn't hurt and is still first on the Rams depth chart, so Corum is projected 9.5.

The quarterback is the other problem, and ThaBrad saw it coming on draft weekend: "wish I took Caleb over hurts. As soon as I pressed the button I regretted." Jalen Hurts is out for the season and C.J. Stroud is on bye, so ThaBrad is starting Jacoby Brissett (17.1) against Josh Allen (24.1). That's a 7-point gap at quarterback, about eight times the projected margin. Brissett's Cardinals play in Dallas, so ThaBrad has his quarterback and CeeDee Lamb on opposite sides of the same game.

Three games that decide it

Tonight, Carolina at Green Bay. Each of them has a Panthers receiver: Jalen Coker for Zeus at 15.24, Tetairoa McMillan for ThaBrad at 15.21. Whoever leads Friday morning will owe it to which Carolina receiver gets the targets.

Sunday, Baltimore at Buffalo. Allen and Moore for Zeus (38.3), Cook and Bass for ThaBrad (25.5). If the Bills score a lot, both of them win this game. If Allen throws the touchdowns and Cook doesn't get the goal-line carries, Zeus wins it.

Sunday, Cleveland at Pittsburgh. Jaylen Warren (15.4) for Zeus against Pat Freiermuth (9.0) for ThaBrad, with Rico Dowdle, another Steeler, on ThaBrad's bench.

The call

Combined, the two lineups project 259.11 points, and the gap is 0.85. Sleeper alone has it 130.27 to 129.79, a 0.48 gap. It's a coin flip either way.

The desk picks the BEAGLES, barely. It isn't about the series. It's about who has backups. Moore and Smith are both questionable, but Zeus has Smith, Tyler Warren, Josh Downs and Kirk Cousins on his bench. If ThaBrad loses a starter, he has Dowdle at 9.1. Zeus also has the last player of the week, Seattle's defense on Monday night. ThaBrad has the better receivers, and he won the last two meetings by 37 and 44. The desk has picked right three weeks in a row. If ThaBrad wins this, Zeus has his first loss, and it was "my year" after all.
