Jarbelo Needs the Cowboys to Beat the Bucs Tonight

Tampa Tropics and The BEAGLES are 1.46 points apart as set. The two loudest men in the chat have been grading each other's rosters since draft night. Zeus leads the series 3–1, and the last time they traded, Zeus won that too.

Oct 8, 2026Tampa Tropics (3–1) vs The BEAGLES (4–0)Week 5 preview

Zeus has written nearly a quarter of every message in this league's chat. Jarbelo is second. On draft night Zeus called Jarbelo's roster "The Could-Bees." After Week 1 Jarbelo answered: "Poorly drafted team." They have not stopped since, and on Sunday they finally settle it on the field.

It starts tonight. The commissioner of the Bucs support group is starting Dak Prescott and the Dallas defense against the Bucs. That's 28.7 of Tampa's projected points riding on the Cowboys beating his own team on Thursday night.

Watch the margins shrink: 40, 37, 11, then the flip. It tracks the two franchises exactly. Zeus went 9–5 and won the 2022 title, then 11–3 and lost the 2023 final. Since then: 7–7, 6–8. Jarbelo went the other way: 4–10, 7–7, 11–3, 12–2. One of them has a ring and a fading record. The other has the best regular season in the league and nothing on his finger.

They have traded once. Week 5, 2024: Jarbelo got Christian McCaffrey, Zeus got Austin Ekeler. McCaffrey scored his first point for Tampa in Week 10. He put up 47.8 for Jarbelo in total, and Jarbelo cut him in Week 14. Ekeler gave Zeus 91.7. Zeus won the deal by 44 points.

Then Zeus drafted McCaffrey fifth overall this year. Jarbelo, after Week 1: "bro you might have drafted a rb2 at #5." Zeus: "Drafted a RB1 in da 7th tho." McCaffrey has scored 13.8, 22.6, 21.6 and 16.0 since. He projects 18.1 against the man who dropped him.

The waiver wire has a grudge in it too. On Sept. 30, Jarbelo put in a claim for Ollie Gordon. So did Zeus, and Zeus won. Gordon scored 18.0 on Zeus's bench in Week 4. On Sunday he's in Zeus's flex, against Tampa.

Tampa Tropics: down bad, open for business

Jarbelo's own scouting report, Wednesday morning: "I'm down bad." Then "RB situation is tough right now." Breece Hall is doubtful, Zach Charbonnet is on PUP, and Jeremiyah Love is questionable. Rico Dowdle, added Wednesday, is questionable on the bench. Tampa has made 25 roster moves, the most in the league. The BEAGLES have made five.

The biggest was Wednesday's trade: Jaxon Smith-Njigba to Peeka Egbuka for Michael Wilson and Bhayshul Tuten. Three days earlier, Zeus had been in the chat complaining about Wilson: "Michael Wilson might ruin McBride." Then: "Why is he getting all his targets!?" Jarbelo went out and bought the targets. Wilson projects 16.0 on Sunday, second only to Justin Jefferson, who is questionable again.

Zeus's review of the deal: "You kind made [Schoolboi] team too much." Jarbelo: "You could have fleeced me if you wanted." Then, to the whole league: "I'm still open for business."

"I have a lot of RBs and I'm tryna level up"

Zeus, Wednesday, the same minute Jarbelo said his RB situation was tough.

One manager is short of running backs. The other is starting four of them and asking for offers. The last time these two traded, it was McCaffrey. Somebody pick up the phone.

Tonight is the Bucs support group's least favourite fixture. Jarbelo, traffikhan and Deion have spent the season mourning the Bucs. Zeus is the man traffikhan busted for being "not a Tampa fan" at every great Tampa sports moment. Zeus's defence: "I may not be a real Tampa fan but I do love me some Rays baseball." Tonight the only Tampa fan in this game is rooting for Dallas. Zeus can finally pull for the Bucs.

The BEAGLES: undefeated and falling apart

The BEAGLES are 4–0 with a 33–11 all-play, second only to TYJT. They have also scored less every single week: 158.56, 146.02, 136.06, 121.62. Keep that line going and Week 5 lands around 109.

The reason is the name. Zeus is an Eagles fan, and his Eagles are in pieces. DeVonta Smith and Dallas Goedert are both questionable and on his bench. Jalen Coker is out. Zeus, Wednesday: "Missing basically the entire offense." Then: "Eagles might be cooked." Deion: "its all unraveling."

Coker was supposed to be the proof. After Week 2 Zeus called Coker and Jonah Coleman "league winners — waiver wire pickups drafted by me!" and added "I had the foresight duud." Jarbelo: "Didn't have the foresight to start Coker yet though." Zeus: "Aktually I did." The record: Coker scored 33.8 on Zeus's bench in Week 1. Started since: 14.6, then 3.8. Now he's out. Coleman is on IR.

So the BEAGLES run on running backs. McCaffrey, Jaylen Warren, Gordon and Rhamondre Stevenson start, two of them in the flex. And they run on Josh Allen: 35.66, 40.82, 17.46, 18.52. "Thank god for Josh Allen," Zeus wrote in Week 1. Allen plays Monday night in Los Angeles. Zeus gets the last word, as usual.

The call

As set at 10:18 Thursday morning, it's 128.64 to 127.18. That's a coin flip with a thumb on it. Four starters are questionable, two on each side, and Tampa has the better insurance: Dowdle at 10.3 against Makai Lemon at 6.8.

The desk picks the BEAGLES. Tampa has the projection, but the BEAGLES have Allen, the better all-play, a 3–1 series and Monday night. The desk also picked Tampa last week and was wrong, and it is not doing that two weeks running. If Dak and the Dallas defense have a big night against the Bucs, Jarbelo gets the lead and gets to sweat it out until Allen is done in Los Angeles. Next week he plays Buttons, the other team on the undefeated mountain. The commish powers have been busy.
