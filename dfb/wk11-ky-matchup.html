The backstory

This rivalry predates every record we have. In September 2013, the league's first season, Zeus started Peyton Manning in Week 1 against Tbunzz. Peyton threw seven touchdowns and 462 yards against the Ravens, which tied the NFL single-game record. At this league's scoring that's 46.48 points. It would rank second in the record book behind Tyreek Hill's 57.90 in 2020. Tbunzz brought it up himself on draft day, twelve years later: his "6pt passing TD trauma from [Zeus] having Peyton in our first year." Nobody has the final score from that game. Everybody remembers who won it.

From 2016 on, they have played 15 times. Zeus leads 8–7. The regular season is 6–5 Zeus. The playoffs are 2–2. They've met in two semifinals, and both times the winner went on to win the title: Zeus by 2.14 in 2017, Tbunzz by 19.12 in 2021.

The Taylords: the champion, fully assembled

Tbunzz started 0–3 again. Then he won seven straight, and the streak hasn't been comfortable. Three of the seven were by 3.02 or less: 0.34 over TYJT in Week 4, 1.32 over Peeka in Week 6, 3.02 over Pukachu in Week 8. He also beat Tampa by 30 last week with a perfect lineup.

Most of this lineup wasn't on his draft board. Tbunzz has made five trades, the most in the league, with five different managers. Mahomes came from JBV in Week 5. St. Brown came in the Week 7 three-way. Emmett Johnson came from traffikhan in Week 8. Odunze and Etienne came from Goon last week. Four of his ten starters arrived by trade, and the deadline is this week.

There's one worry. Ken Walker is questionable. He plays for Kansas City now, with Mahomes and Emmett Johnson. If Walker sits, Braelon Allen (8.9) is the backup. Waddle and Hockenson are also questionable on the bench.

The BEAGLES: four for four on the big stage

Zeus won the 2022 title and lost the 2023 final to Goon. This year he started 7–0, lost two straight, then beat TYJT by 30 last week in this slot. He has the best quarterback in the game, Josh Allen (24.5), and the best running back, Christian McCaffrey (19.9).

The receivers are the problem, and the news has been bad all week. Emeka Egbuka, the receiver Zeus traded Jonah Coleman for, plays at Detroit, and on Wednesday Zeus reported the bad news himself: "Looks like Baker is out." Then: "Not official." Then: "Foot strain." ThaBrad had just asked who was "picking up the lions D for against Tampa?! Have you no soul?" That was Zeus, and the Lions defense is now on his bench while a bye-week Seattle starts.

Josh Downs is the other storyline. On deadline day Zeus watched him get traded to Kansas City and posted "Oh fuuuuuck Downs just got Mahomes." Downs starts in Zeus's flex this week, catching passes from Tbunzz's quarterback. A Mahomes-to-Downs touchdown is points for both sides.

The call

As set, Sleeper has the Taylords by 17.1. If Zeus fixes his defense and flex, it's about 5.5. A simulation that uses each team's actual scoring this season makes the BEAGLES a slight favorite, about 55%, because they've averaged 136.0 a week to the Taylords' 132.6.

The desk picks the Taylords. Tbunzz has every slot filled with a starter who plays, Zeus's Egbuka may not have his quarterback, and Tbunzz has won the last two meetings by 51.66 and 96.92. The BEAGLES are 4–0 in this slot, and the desk picked them all four times. This is the first time it has picked against them.

Also on the slate: Rage vs JohnnyBoVonny

The undercard is the last game this season between the only two teams never featured here. JBV is 0–10, the first team in league history to get there. Goon is 3–7 and has won three straight. They've played six times since 2019, and JBV leads 5–1. JBV has won the last four, 2022 through 2025.

Sleeper likes the winless team. JBV projects 125.5 to Rage's 118.6, mostly because Rage is starting Aaron Rodgers and Ladd McConkey for Jordan Love and Drake London, both on bye. The simulation goes the other way and gives Rage about 65%, because JBV has averaged 107.6 a week, the lowest in the league.

Both lineups have one thing in common: Tbunzz. JBV's Jayden Daniels and Germie Bernard came from Tbunzz, and Justin Jefferson came in the same three-way. Rage's McConkey and Rachaad White came from Tbunzz last week. The champion has a hand in every game on this page.

JBV's brother has defended him: "Team did fine 2 weeks in a row. Just got smacked." Zeus has not: "[JBV] 0-10 rostering 3 defenses what the actual fuck lol." JBV is down to two, Buffalo and Tampa Bay, and starts Buffalo. If he loses this one, he has three left to avoid 0–14. The simulation puts 0–14 at 17%.

Goon's playoff hopes are gone. Last week he said "My playoff chances are 6% if i win out which is obviously going to happen." The simulation now has Rage under 0.1%. The desk picks Rage, and is taking no questions.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 11 PPR projections, summed over each starting lineup as set at 8:01 p.m. ET Thursday, Nov 19. Injury tags and bye weeks are Sleeper's at the same time. The series covers every meeting between the two managers on ESPN (2016–2021, playoffs included) and Sleeper (2022–2025, regular season); the 2013 Peyton game predates the records and is not counted. Playoff and bye chances come from 100,000 simulations of Weeks 11–14 using the real remaining schedule, each team's weekly score drawn from the mean and spread of its ten 2026 scores, six playoff teams, top two get byes, ties broken by points. Chat quotes are from the league chat, with real names swapped for nicknames.
