Rage went 3–0 in 2024, then ran into a wall. Six opponents cleared 150 on him, three of them in the last six weeks: 160, 181, 183. Rage outscored the league average that year and still finished 6–8. Against the whole league every week, his scores went 80–74, worth about seven wins. The schedule took at least one off him. That was the second half of the 3–0 curse, and the week Tua came back is still a sore subject.

Adjust for era and the crown moves to the ESPN years. In 2019 the man who won the title a year later had opponents score 22 points a game above the league average on him. He went 4–9, then won it all in 2020. Some years the schedule is the whole story.

This year is coming for the list

Three of the top four raw seasons are happening right now. Schoolboi, JBV and Jov have each faced about 139 a game, 11 to 12 above the league. They're a combined 5–25. JBV is 0–10 while drawing the second-hardest schedule in the league, so the 0–14 watch is at least partly not his fault. Four games are left for any of them to catch 147.5. It would take about 170 a week against them.

Was traffikhan right?

"[JBV]/[Zeus] are extremely lucky and [Goudariddance]/[Tbunzz] are unlucky af"

traffikhan, Sept. 6, the night the all-play numbers came out

Half right, and the half that's right is very right. Across 145 regular-season games, Zeus's opponents have scored 3.7 a game below the league average, the easiest schedule of any long-time manager. It held in his title year, 2022 (−6.6). It's holding now: 118 a game against, second-easiest in the league, and he's 8–2.

JBV is the miss. His career is dead even (−0.8), and this season the schedule is the reason he's winless. Goudariddance checks out: two of the eight worst schedules ever. Tbunzz's bad luck isn't the schedule (−0.8 career). It's when the big weeks land, which the all-play already told everybody.

How this was put together

Every regular-season game since 2016: ESPN export for 2016–21, Sleeper for 2022 on, ESPN managers matched to their Sleeper accounts. Playoff and consolation games are left out. "vs lg" is a team's average points against minus the league's average score that season. 2013–15 aren't in any export. The 2026 numbers stop at Week 10; Week 11 is still being played.
