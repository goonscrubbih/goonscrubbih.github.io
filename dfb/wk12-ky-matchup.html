K&Y Matchup of the Week · Week 12

Locked In Since September

On draft day ThaBrad told the league "Yall are fucked." He had the worst record of the Sleeper era. Now he's 8–3, has won six straight, and is one win from the best season he's ever had. traffikhan has beaten him four straight, opening night included, and needs this one to keep a bye alive.

Nov 25, 2026Sex Panthers (8–3) vs TYJT (7–4)Week 12 preview

On draft day, Buttons asked whether ThaBrad even knew what day it was and told him to set up his auto draft. An hour later ThaBrad checked in: "Oh I'm locked in baby. Yall are fucked." He came into the season 20–36 on Sleeper, the worst record of the era, and opened it with a 51-point loss to traffikhan. At 2–3 nobody was afraid of anybody.

He hasn't lost since. The Sex Panthers are 8–3, second in the standings and second in points. TYJT went the other way. traffikhan was 7–2 and first in points two weeks ago. Then he lost to the BEAGLES and to Buttons, and he's 7–4. If ThaBrad wins, his chance of a first-round bye goes to 66%. If he loses, it's 26%. traffikhan needs the win just to keep one alive: 18% with it, 0.2% without.

The backstory

They've played eight times, and traffikhan leads 5–3. But for most of their history they weren't in the league together. ThaBrad joined in 2018. traffikhan left after that season and didn't come back until 2023. So 2018 is the only ESPN season they share, and it was all ThaBrad. He beat traffikhan twice, by 50 and by 14, while traffikhan finished 3–10, 12th of 12. Their team names that year were "No mo Kareem Cheese" and "No Mas Leche." One of them had a better season.

Since traffikhan came back, he's 5–1. The one he lost was the ugliest game in the series. In Week 2 of 2024, neither team reached 90, and ThaBrad won by 1.18 because his kicker scored 15. The kicker was Evan McPherson. McPherson kicks for traffikhan now. He isn't the only one who switched sides. James Cook scored 26.5 and 18.7 against ThaBrad for traffikhan last season and now leads ThaBrad's running backs. Christian Watson sat on ThaBrad's bench the first time they met on Sleeper, in 2023. He's traffikhan's top scorer this year.

Opening night was the low point. traffikhan scored 156.60, the third-best score of the week, with Watson at 32.7. ThaBrad got 24.72 from Jalen Hurts, 15.4 from Lamb, and 3.20 from George Kittle. ThaBrad's review: "0-1! Boyyyssss haha."

They don't talk to each other much in the chat. Their best exchange was in October, about the 0–4 Texans. traffikhan: "Only one 0-4 team has made the playoffs in NFL history." ThaBrad: "so you're saying there's a chance." The Texans are 4–6 now, and their quarterback is ThaBrad's.

They do share a team. ThaBrad is a Bucs fan, quietly. Last week, when Zeus reported that Baker Mayfield had a foot strain, ThaBrad wrote "Thank god we paid him." traffikhan is the loud one. He's spent the season on Baker, and one of his lines is "There's a legit argument that Baker has singlehandedly lost us three games." This matchup ends with the Bucs on Monday night, and the last player in the game is traffikhan's Bucky Irving.

Sex Panthers: the quarterback the desk picked for him

ThaBrad drafted Hurts and regretted it right away: "wish I took Caleb over hurts. As soon as I pressed the button I regretted." On Sept 17 the desk's Week 2 preview called his team "the worst franchise in this league's history." Deion: "dam the AI putting [ThaBrad]'s team on blast." At 2:10 p.m. ThaBrad answered, "Not the computer! How will I recover." At 2:19 he picked up C.J. Stroud.

He benched Hurts for Stroud in Week 5, when Hurts was still healthy. Hurts was hurt the next week and is on injured reserve. Since the switch, the Sex Panthers are 6–1. Their 155.36 last week was the top score in the league, with a perfect lineup, and it would have beaten all eleven teams. CeeDee Lamb had 30.2 of it, and his 252.2 points as a starter are the most on either roster.

He's had the schedule's help too. He called last week's game before it happened: "pretty happy with the apparent scheduled waxing." He beat Deion by 25. In the same message he predicted traffikhan's loss: "[Buttons] might get a free dub against [traffikhan] too. Byes and injuries." Buttons won by 17.

The record is the story. ThaBrad's best Sleeper season was 6–8. His best ever were 8–6 on ESPN in 2018 and 2019. Both years he lost his first playoff game to Goudariddance. One more win and it's the best season he's ever had, with three weeks still to play.

TYJT: the quarterback coin flip

traffikhan hasn't posted in the chat since Nov. 15, the Sunday of the first of his two losses. His roster has done the talking.

He has two starting quarterbacks, Brock Purdy and Trevor Lawrence, and he picks one every week. He's picked the lower scorer three times. Last week he started Lawrence (12.74) and benched Purdy (22.2). He also started Juwan Johnson at tight end for 0.0. He lost by 16.62, and the Week 11 Review gave him Fraud Watch. On Tuesday he dropped Juwan. That ends "Juwan for Burden," the trade he offered Jarbelo twice in September. It never happened.

He's also the busiest manager in this game: 37 completed moves to ThaBrad's 17. One of them hurt. traffikhan added Emmett Johnson in Week 2, cut him, re-added him, and traded him to Tbunzz in Week 8 for Jadarian Price. Since the trade, Emmett has scored 94.1 for the Taylords. Price has scored 28.9 for TYJT and sits on the bench this week.

This week it's Lawrence again, projected 22.8, over a questionable Purdy at 16.2. Purdy's 49ers play Seattle at 4:25. Lawrence's Jaguars play Tennessee at 1:00. Whatever traffikhan picks, he'll know by Sunday afternoon whether it was right.

"in a league like ours luck is what wins"

traffikhan, Sept. 6. ThaBrad's 8–3 is no accident: he's 75–46 against the whole league, third-best.

Where it gets decided

Wednesday night, Green Bay at the Rams. Already under way. Corum for ThaBrad. In October he said Corum "is just one kyren shredded chicken from becoming CMC." Kyren Williams is healthy and already has a touchdown. Watson goes for traffikhan.

Sunday at 1:00, New Orleans at Cincinnati. Tee Higgins for ThaBrad, McPherson for traffikhan. Same offense, so a Higgins drive that stalls turns into McPherson points.

Sunday at 4:25, Seattle at San Francisco. Kittle for ThaBrad against traffikhan's 49ers defense. Purdy, probably on traffikhan's bench, throws to Kittle.

Monday night, Carolina at Tampa Bay. Tetairoa McMillan for ThaBrad, Bucky Irving for traffikhan, and Chris Godwin on ThaBrad's bench. Two Bucs fans, one Bucs running back, last game of the week.

The call

As set, Sleeper has TYJT by 7.1. A simulation that uses each team's actual scoring this season makes the Sex Panthers a slight favorite, about 54%. They've averaged 137.7 a week to TYJT's 133.2, and TYJT's last two were 110.76 and 99.64.

The desk picks the Sex Panthers. ThaBrad is on a six-game streak with a perfect lineup last week. traffikhan has two questionable starters and lost his last two. traffikhan has won four straight in the series, and the last time ThaBrad won one, he needed a kicker who now plays for the other side. The desk went 2–for–2 last week. Regression says this is the one it gets wrong.

Correction: The Week 11 Review said ThaBrad went 8–5 on ESPN in 2018 and 2019. Both seasons were 8–6. One more win is still a career high.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 12 PPR projections, summed over each starting lineup as set at 9:01 p.m. ET Wednesday, Nov 25. Injury tags are Sleeper's at the same time. The series covers every meeting between the two managers on ESPN (2016–2021) and Sleeper (2022–2026); they were in the league together only in 2018 and since 2023. Quarterback picks, move counts and the Emmett Johnson tally come from Sleeper's weekly scoring and transaction log. "Against the whole league" is each team's record if it had played every other team every week. Playoff and bye chances come from 100,000 simulations of Weeks 12–14 on the real remaining schedule, each team's weekly score drawn from the mean and spread of its eleven 2026 scores, six playoff teams, top two get byes, ties broken by points. Chat quotes are from the league chat, with real names swapped for nicknames.
