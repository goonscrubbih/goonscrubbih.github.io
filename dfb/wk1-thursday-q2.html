Purdy Throws It To Pukachu

Brock Purdy's interception at 12:04 of the second quarter is worth two points to the Rams defense — enough to push I Choose You Pukachu! past JohnnyBoVonny's 16.8 for the first time tonight.

Sept 10, 2026 · 9:18 PM ET LAR 7, SF 3 · 12:04 Q2

Before kickoff the preview asked one question: could a Puka Nacua and Rams-defense stack match the 16.82 JohnnyBoVonny got from the same kind of bet on Wednesday? At the end of the first quarter it was 1.3 short. Three minutes into the second, San Francisco handed it the difference.

Purdy threw short right for George Kittle from the Rams 46, and Quentin Lake picked it off at the Rams 31. In this league a defensive interception is worth two points. That takes the Rams defense from 9.0 to 11.0 and Pukachu from 15.5 to 17.5.

Nobody in this league started Purdy

The interception cost nobody a starter's points. Purdy sits on Team Traffikhan's bench, where he was already putting up a quiet 12-of-18 for 80 yards. The pass was meant for Kittle — Sex Panthers' starter, who has five targets and two catches.

So the only fantasy consequence of the play is the one Pukachu was waiting on. The defense's number moves, Puka's stays at 6.5 from two catches and 45 yards, and the bet that looked 1.3 short at the quarter break is 0.7 ahead.

Two things keep it from being settled. The stack still has more than a half to play, and every point San Francisco scores from here can move the defense back down. And for a few minutes the app disagreed: Sleeper's live feed was slow to count the interception, still showing 15.5 for Pukachu. By 9:22 PM it had caught up — 11.0 for the defense, 17.5 for Pukachu, exactly what the league's scoring settings said it would be.

Nacua Matata pulls away

The other scoring play of the night came one snap into the second quarter: Kyren Williams from five yards out, finishing the drive Puka's 41-yard catch had set up. Williams is at 9.8, and Nacua Matata leads the night's only head-to-head 22.4 to 7.4.

DeionSandalz's lone 49er is doing everything asked of him — Mike Evans has caught all four of his targets for 34 yards — and it barely registers against a lead built on a Wednesday kicker and a Thursday touchdown. Davante Adams, the other Ram in Nacua Matata's lineup, still has one target and nothing else.
