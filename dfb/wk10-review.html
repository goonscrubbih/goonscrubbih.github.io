There was a wall before 200. Buttons went through it.

Back in Week 5 Buttons scored 197.36 and wrote: "There's like a wall before 200 in this league." He was 1.42 short of Jarbelo's record. In ten years nobody had scored 200, on ESPN or on Sleeper. The best ESPN score was Jarbelo's 195.78 in 2018.

On Sunday night he asked the chat, "Will I hit the wall or break the record!?" traffikhan: "You already broke the record." Jahmyr Gibbs scored 44.2, George Pickens 43.5, Isaiah Likely 27.7, Tony Pollard 23.9, and the Minnesota defense 22. Zeus, about Ryan Flournoy's late touchdown: "Holy SPIT." Buttons finished at 220.00, 21.22 points past the old record.

It could have been more. He started Bryce Young (8.1) and benched Kirk Cousins (15.38). Zeus: "Why didn't you start Kirk?!?" Buttons: "lol I didn't start Bryce last week and started Kirk they keep flip flopping." His best lineup was 229.08. Then he went after Jarbelo: "J shoulda just kept jsn would be in the running. Can't get over it." Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 31.1 this week for Peeka. Jarbelo traded him in Week 5.

Deion scored 107.86 and lost by 112. Joe Burrow scored 33.16, and his tight end, Darren Waller, scored 4.4. On Wednesday morning Deion dropped Waller and picked up Mark Andrews, the player he cut on Nov 3. That day traffikhan called cutting Andrews "the more compassionate option." Buttons leads the series 8–5.

Six and six

Buttons said it Tuesday: "Top 6 this year is gapped from the bottom. Usually everyone is like 1 game apart." The numbers agree with him. Six teams are 7–3 or better. The best of the rest is 4–6. From 2016 to 2025 the gap between sixth and seventh after ten weeks was never bigger than one game. This year it's three.

Four weeks are left. We ran the rest of the season 100,000 times, with each team's 2026 scoring and ties broken by points. Buttons and the BEAGLES made the playoffs in effectively every run, and the other four top teams made it in 95% or more. Tampa is at 10%. Pukachu is at 1%. The bye is the real race now: Buttons 81%, the BEAGLES 63%, TYJT 21%, Sex Panthers 18%, Deion 10%, the Taylords 8%.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Nov 18 after Week 10 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. "vs. league" is each team's record if it had played every other team every week. Records, series and history cover ESPN (2016–2021) and Sleeper (2022–2026).

Playoff odds come from 100,000 simulations of Weeks 11–14 on the real schedule. Each team's weekly score is drawn from a normal distribution with its 2026 mean and spread. Six teams make it, the top two get byes, and ties go to points. Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets.
