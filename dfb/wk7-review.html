The mountain comes down to one

Three weeks ago Zeus said he and Buttons "stand on the undefeated mountain." On Sunday they played each other, and only Zeus is still up there. The BEAGLES won 136.40–126.12. They are 7–0, the last unbeaten team in the league.

Zeus did it without Josh Allen, who was on bye. He started Kirk Cousins, the Wednesday pickup he named "Kirko Chainz," and Cousins scored 20.4. Rhamondre Stevenson scored 22.5 on Thursday night, Christian McCaffrey 22.0 and Ollie Gordon 17.3. Every slot held the best player Zeus had, so he left 0.00 on his bench. 136.40 was the top score of the week, 0.60 ahead of Pukachu, and it beat all eleven teams. Zeus, Tuesday morning: "No Josh Allen — TOP SCORE." Back in Week 6 he called a top score and finished third. This time he got it.

Buttons's roster could have won by 30.8. Tony Pollard scored 36.5 on his bench, more than any player in the NFL this week. Buttons flexed DeMario Douglas (5.3) again, and Harold Fannin scored 13.1 on the bench as well. Buttons left 41.10 points on his bench, the most in the league. His best lineup was 167.22, which would have been the week's top score by 30.8. Zeus spotted it before the early games ended: "Pollard on [Buttons]'s bench thank god."

Josh Jacobs did come back. "Jacob's back baby," Buttons said late Saturday night, and Jacobs scored 20.1. Isaiah Likely did not pop: 3.2. Zeus had bet against him that night: "Likely to not w McCarthy." Zeus took a 16-point lead into Monday night, Dallas Goedert against George Pickens, for what he called "the Golden Lube." Pickens won that duel 15.7–9.8. Buttons needed three times that.

The series is now 10–9 Zeus over 19 meetings, ESPN and Sleeper, including Zeus's win in the 2017 ESPN final. The desk changed its pick to the BEAGLES after Thursday night. That makes three Matchup of the Week picks right in a row.

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Oct 27 after Week 7 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. Defense points use this league's scoring settings.

Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets. Transactions are from the league's own log. This league uses rolling waivers, not FAAB, so there are no bids to report.
