The hamstring

Zay Flowers scored 26.00 in Week 1, the Taylords' second-best starter. He is listed questionable with a hamstring, and Tbunzz has him on the bench as of this writing, with Matthew Golden starting in his place.

That single decision is the matchup. Measured on each roster's best available lineup, these two teams are three points apart — the closest game of the week. Measured on the lineups as they are actually set right now, the Sex Panthers lead by 35, almost all of it the difference between Flowers and Golden at one receiver slot.

So there are two versions of this game. In one, the defending champion fields his real team and it is a coin flip. In the other, he protects a hamstring in mid-September and hands a 35-point edge to the team with the worst record in league history.

How this one got picked

Every Week 2 game was scored on combined roster strength and how close the two sides are, then weighed against head-to-head history and what is at stake. This one came out on top at 94.9: the smallest gap on the board by a wide margin, and the only game where both teams are 0–1.

The runner-up was The BEAGLES vs Rage at 86.3 — the better rosters, and a series Rage leads 4–2 including a 172.6–95.3 playoff win last December. Rage is this desk's own team, so for the record: it lost on the numbers, not on the optics. A 55-point roster gap against a 3-point one is not close.

The Taylords also appear here for the second week running, which is worth saying out loud. They earned it by losing the closest game of Week 1 and drawing the tightest matchup of Week 2 — not by being the team this desk finds most interesting.
