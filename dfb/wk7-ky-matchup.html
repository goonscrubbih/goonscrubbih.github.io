Nine Wins Each. Only One Stays Perfect.

Oct 22, 2026The BEAGLES (6–0) vs Benjamin's Buttons (6–0)Week 7 preview

There was never a debate this week. On Oct 5 Zeus announced it from the summit: "Me and [Buttons] stand on the undefeated mountain." This Sunday the two of them play each other. One leaves 7–0 and the other gets his first loss.

Zeus talked about it on Wednesday, with his quarterback on a bye: "I'm so ready to douse [Buttons]'s ass in KY even though Josh Allen on a bye!" Buttons answered by describing his own lineup: "I'll be watching Gibbs fresh off bye and Jacob's running off his frustrations in the same game."

The backstory

They have played 18 times and split them nine apiece. The split is not random. Buttons is 8–6 in the regular season. Zeus is 3–1 in the playoffs.

The ESPN years (2016–2021) and the Sleeper years (2022 on), every meeting. The 2017 final is the only title game they have played against each other.

That 2017 final is the best argument in this preview for Zeus. It was an ugly one, 87.12 to 82.06, on ESPN. Buttons won the title the next year, and each of them has two rings now (Zeus 2017 and 2022, Buttons 2018 and 2024). They have not met in a final since.

The regular season tells the opposite story. Buttons won six straight regular-season meetings, from Week 2 of 2018 to Week 3 of 2022, including a 56-point demolition in 2020 (156.62 to 100.40) and a 3.56-point squeaker in 2018. Zeus has won three of the last five, including 139.12 to 97.18 last October. Their closest regular-season game is that 3.56 in 2018. The biggest is the 56.22 in 2020.

Week 7 of a 14-week regular season is not a playoff game. It is the one that hands one of them his first loss of the year.

The BEAGLES: win big, lose the quarterback

Zeus is 6–0 with the second-most points in the league (867.7) and a 52–14 record against everyone. He has never won by fewer than 11 points. His six margins: 30.66, 11.24, 31.92, 17.12, 89.28 and 18.99, an average of 33. After Week 4's slow 121.62 he put up 173.00 on Tampa, then 132.40 on Iceman Williams.

This week he does it with Kirk Cousins, whom he picked up Wednesday and renamed "Kirko Chainz." Josh Allen is on bye. Cousins projects 15.5 against Buttons's Bryce Young at 18.6, a 3.1-point hole at quarterback before anyone kicks off. DJ Moore is out.

"Might've drafted an RB2 in the first but drafted a RB1 in multiple other rounds muhahahahaah"

Zeus, Oct 14. McCaffrey is projected 21.1 on Sunday.

Zeus runs on his backs: McCaffrey (21.1), Warren (16.9), and Ollie Gordon and Rhamondre Stevenson in the flex. Stevenson, "RHammer," plays Thursday night, Patriots at Bears, opposite Buttons's D'Andre Swift. The one problem is the Eagles. Jalen Hurts is out for the season, DeVonta Smith and Dallas Goedert are in the lineup anyway, and both play Monday night against Dallas. Zeus, two weeks ago: "Missing basically the entire offense… Eagles might be cooked."

Zeus has history with quarterbacks. In the league's first season, Week 1 of 2013, he started Peyton Manning for seven touchdown passes against Tbunzz, who is still "dealing with the 6pt passing TD trauma." This week his quarterback is Kirk Cousins.

Benjamin's Buttons: win small, win anyway

Buttons is the other 6–0 team, with 849.0 points and a 44–22 all-play record, and he wins differently. Four of his six wins came by 10.22 or fewer, and one came by 0.08. His average margin is 18. In Week 4 he beat Goudariddance by 0.08, and Bryce Young's last kneel-down made the difference: "If Bryce would have kneeled one more time I would have lost." A week later he scored 197.36, 1.42 off the league record, and said: "There's like a wall before 200 in this league."

His quarterback Sunday is that same Bryce Young, projected 18.6, and he has Gibbs back from the bye at 25.2, the highest projection on the field. Isaiah Likely, the subject of Buttons's "Likely to POP" chant, is at tight end. Josh Jacobs, a dead roster spot for most of the year, is in the flex at 11.8, in the same game as Gibbs. Buttons, Oct 17: "Just found out Jacob's is back 😈"

He is also the league's waiver machine. Buttons has made 16 roster moves this season to Zeus's 10, and in September traffikhan did the math on him: "[Buttons] actually destroys everyone else in waivers. Over 1k points through waivers is nuts." Early Thursday he added D. Douglas and dropped M. Fields. He wins small and he wins constantly.

The call

As set at 1:31 Thursday morning, it's 137.07 to 135.01. That is a coin flip with a thumb on it. Buttons has the quarterback, Gibbs, Likely and the better defense. Zeus has the deeper running game, Coker, and the 2017 final.

Thursday night comes first: Swift for Buttons against Stevenson for Zeus, projected 15.6 to 11.1, which is a 4.5-point head start for Buttons before the weekend. Then Monday night: Smith and Goedert for Zeus against Pickens for Buttons, 24.8 to 11.7. That is 13 points of projected cushion for Zeus on the last night. Zeus gets the last word.

The desk picks Buttons. The desk was wrong on Tampa in Week 4 and right in Weeks 5 and 6, and this one is closer than either. The edge is the quarterback gap and the bye: Zeus is playing without Josh Allen and Buttons is getting Gibbs back. Either way, one 6–0 team becomes 6–1 on Sunday, and the other is 7–0 with a very loud chat.
