Winner Gets the Bye

traffikhan has the most points in the league. Zeus has the best record against everyone. Both are 7–2, both are all but in, and Sunday decides who is favored for a week off in December.

Nov 12, 2026TYJT (7–2) vs The BEAGLES (7–2)Week 10 preview

Four teams are 7–2. Two of them play each other this week. The playoffs are not really in question: each of these teams makes it in about 99 of every 100 simulations of the rest of the season. What's in question is the bye. Six teams make the playoffs and the top two sit out the first round.

Right now traffikhan has the best chance at one of those two spots, about 53%. Zeus is at 44%. Win on Sunday and either of them goes to roughly 70%. Lose and it drops to between 25% and 32%. This is the biggest swing in the league this week.

traffikhan settled on Zeus's reputation before the season started: "[JBV]/[Zeus] are extremely lucky." He had a point then. Zeus was 33–23 on Sleeper with a .491 all-play, the luckiest good record in the league. He doesn't have the point now. This season Zeus is 70–29 against everyone, the best mark in the league, and his 7–2 is if anything a little unlucky.

The other running argument is about Tampa. traffikhan is a founding member of the Bucs support group, and he has called Zeus "not a Tampa fan" at every great Tampa sports moment. Zeus's defense: "I may not be a real Tampa fan but I do love me some Rays." This week the Bucs are on bye, and so is traffikhan's Bucky Irving.

TYJT: from "actually trash" to first in points

Before the season traffikhan's three Sleeper finishes were 11th, 2nd and 12th, and he summed it up himself: "nah I'm actually trash." The 2nd was 2024, when he reached the final and lost to Buttons. Last year he went 5–9. His career record on Sleeper was 16–26.

This year he has 1,254.7 points, the most in the league, and he's 66–33 against everyone. Last week he scored 173.98, the top score of the week, and beat Tampa with Christian Watson (30.1), Bucky Irving (28.5) and Brock Purdy (25.28). His bench quarterback, Trevor Lawrence, projects 21.6, within two points of his starter.

"[Tbunzz] started 1-4 last year and ended the season with 9 straight wins and a chip"

traffikhan, Tuesday. He was answering ThaBrad, and he knows how seasons turn.

The weak spot is the second running back. Bucky is on bye, so Jadarian Price (8.1) and Aaron Jones (8.9) fill in, and Omar Cooper is questionable in the flex. The strength is Brock Bowers (16.2), nearly four points better than Zeus's tight end.

The BEAGLES: off the mountain

Zeus won the 2022 title and lost the 2023 final to Goon. This year he started 7–0, the last team unbeaten. Then he lost to ThaBrad by 13.60 and to Deion by 49.58, scoring 107.96 and 107.26, his two lowest scores of the year. His post-game report on Tuesday: "My undefeated days hath ended now on a losing streak!"

He also had a rough Sunday off the field. At 3:30 he posted "Currently a JT and Fannin TD away from $500." An hour later: "Only one not to hit… JT." Jonathan Taylor starts for traffikhan on Sunday, projected 17.3. Zeus will be rooting against him again.

Zeus's lineup is better on paper. Josh Allen (26.3) is the best-projected player in the game, and Christian McCaffrey (22.2) is third, behind Purdy. Both halves of the lineup he named in September are starting: Jaylen Warren and Tyler Warren, "Attorneys at BALL," project 29.5 together. His weak spot is the Egbuka trade. Egbuka is on bye, Goedert is on bye, and Adonai Mitchell (7.8) starts in the flex.

Where it gets decided

Sunday at 1:00, Buffalo at the Jets. Josh Allen and DJ Moore for Zeus, with Shakir on his bench. Both teams start a Jets receiver: Omar Cooper for traffikhan, Adonai Mitchell for Zeus.

Sunday at 1:00, Miami at Indianapolis. Jonathan Taylor for traffikhan against Tyler Warren and Ollie Gordon for Zeus. Same field, Zeus's parlay ghost on the other side.

Sunday at 4:25, San Francisco at Dallas. traffikhan starts Brock Purdy and the 49ers defense. Zeus starts Christian McCaffrey. Every McCaffrey touchdown is good for Zeus and fine for Purdy. A McCaffrey fumble helps nobody.

Sunday night, Pittsburgh at Cincinnati. The last players in the game: Jaylen Warren for Zeus and Evan McPherson for traffikhan.

The call

Sleeper has the BEAGLES by 13.8. A simulation that uses each team's actual scoring this season makes traffikhan a slight favorite, about 55%, because he has scored 35 more points than Zeus this year and Zeus is coming off his two worst weeks.

The desk picks the BEAGLES. Allen is the best-projected player in this game, McCaffrey is close behind, both Warrens start, and traffikhan is without Bucky. The desk has been wrong two weeks running, so take that for what it's worth.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 10 PPR projections, summed over each starting lineup as set at 1:35 p.m. ET Thursday, Nov 12. Injury tags and bye weeks are Sleeper's at the same time. The series covers every meeting between the two managers on ESPN (2016–2021) and Sleeper (2022–2025), eight in all. Playoff and bye chances come from 100,000 simulations of Weeks 10–14 using the real schedule. Each team's weekly score is drawn from its own 2026 average and spread, with ties in the standings broken by total points. Six teams make the playoffs and the top two get a bye. These are estimates from nine weeks of scoring and they don't know about injuries. Quotes are from the league chat, verbatim; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets.
