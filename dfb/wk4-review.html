Fraud Watch, Week 4: the kneel-down

Sunday night, Buttons led Pukachu by a sliver and needed Bryce Young to stop doing anything. Their manager narrated the finish in the chat: "If it's -.20 each kneel I'm fucked." Then, after the clock ran out: "If Bryce would have kneeled one more time I would have lost." Then a stat correction moved it another tenth and he was sweating all over again. Final margin: 0.08.

Buttons are 4–0, seventh in points, and 22–22 against the whole league. That is the profile of a 2–2 team. Kyle Monangai scored 28.0 on their bench.

Pukachu's best lineup was 163.18. Brian Robinson scored 25.7 on the bench while David Montgomery started and scored 4.3. That swap alone wins by 21. Goudariddance already owns the worst luck in league history, and this week he added to the lead.

"If Bryce would have kneeled one more time I would have lost."

Buttons, 4–0, on the week's deciding play

ThaBrad was right. It did boom.

On Sept 17 ThaBrad told the chat his team was "boom or bust" and would "boom any week now." Week 4: Tetairoa McMillan scored 45.2, CeeDee Lamb 41.3, and the Sex Panthers put up 191.32, the fourth-highest score since the league moved to Sleeper. They beat all eleven teams, made two roster moves all season, and went 2–2.

The bad news for ThaBrad is where he spent it. His own review, at 1 a.m.: "Can't believe I wasted two 40 burgers on rage."

Rage scored 100.52, the lowest of the week. Ja'Marr Chase played through a questionable tag for 5.7, Dalton Kincaid scored 1.7, the kicker 2.0. Rage's best possible lineup was 121.62, which loses by 70. No lineup excuse this time, just a beating. Buttons, in the chat, described it with less restraint than the desk is allowed.

Two pieces of good news, though. Sunday morning Rage traded Garrett Wilson to Jov for Chuba Hubbard and Malik Washington. Hubbard scored 25.9, Rage's best starter, and Wilson scored 5.7. Jov, that night: "I got fleeced!!" And Rage finally dropped the Raiders. The Packers scored 10, the Raiders zero. The defense ledger's first win came the week it couldn't matter.

The Matchup of the Week: the desk got it wrong

Last Wednesday the desk picked Tampa in a game projected 0.04 apart, citing a 26-point floor for Jaxon Smith-Njigba and history: Jov had never beaten Tampa before Week 10. JSN scored 12.6. Justin Jefferson scored zero on the bench. Kenyon Sadiq scored zero at tight end. Jov won by 10, and the call was wrong.

Kyren Williams scored 36.7, and Alvin Kamara added 22.8 on Monday night to finish it. Kamara is on his third roster this season, and he picked this one to show up. The series is 5–3 Jov. On Oct 1, Jov conceded this one in advance and promised his "revenge in the playoffs." He got it early.

Then Jov did the cruelest thing anyone has done in this league this season: on Monday morning he picked up Kyle Pitts, the tight end Jarbelo drafted drunk and cut a week ago. Jov said Pitts wanted to "look [Jarbelo] in the face and laugh at his new TEs goose egg." Jarbelo's reply: "I hope Kamara gets hurt. And Pitts knee explodes." Zeus ruled it "Salty."

Tampa's Wednesday response was move number 25 of the season, the most in the league by three: Smith-Njigba to Peeka Egbuka for Michael Wilson and Bhayshul Tuten. That is half of Jarbelo's "60 from jj and jsn" plan, traded. The plan produced 12.6 this week.

The champion is 1–3

"Our champion is 0–3" is retired. The Taylords beat TYJT by 0.34, on 30.9 from Kenneth Walker and 25.8 from Zay Flowers. Parker Washington, the receiver Tbunzz defends against all comers, scored 2.0. Emanuel Wilson, picked up Friday, scored 27.0 on the bench.

Buttons jinxed it Sunday night by congratulating Tbunzz with Monday still to play, then added that Tbunzz "started 0–5 last year and won it all." He started 1–4. Close enough; the point stands. traffikhan: "I pray you just jinxed him." He did not.

TYJT's best lineup was 151.72, and Khalil Shakir's 13.2 on the bench would have covered it. traffikhan is still first in points and 36–8 against the field, the best in the league by three games. Juwan Johnson, the player he has offered Tampa for Luther Burden twice, scored 11.9. Burden scored 11.4. That trade is still a coin flip.

Around the league

The BEAGLES are 4–0, and Zeus has been telling everyone: "Me and Ben stand on the undefeated mountain." Will Reichard kicked for 19.0 and Rhamondre Stevenson added 18.4 from the flex. Four roster moves all season.

JohnnyBoVonny started Patrick Mahomes (17.0) over Drake Maye (26.16) and lost by 17. In Week 2 he benched Mahomes and lost by 2.5. JBV is 0–4 and 7–37 against the field, from the luckiest career record in league history. His best lineup, 139.56, beats Zeus. Zeus: "Will Reichard sends lil [JBV] to the Shadow Realm."

Stay Questionable got 31.3 from Javonte Williams in the flex and 27.7 from Bijan. On Sept 8 Deion said "We making playoffs this year." At 3–1 and 31–13 against the field, that looks right. He was also the only team to claim Tyreek Hill.

Peeka Egbuka started Sam Darnold, quarterback number four, over Geno Smith, and got the call right. It didn't matter. On Wednesday Schoolboi traded for JSN and then posted in the chat for the fifth time ever. The message, in full: "no more Lane."

How this was put together

Scores, lineups and every rostered player's points come from Sleeper, pulled Oct 7 after Week 4 closed. "Best possible lineup" fills each slot with the highest scorer on the roster, using hindsight on purpose. Kyle Pitts, added by Chubba on Monday after Atlanta had played, is left out of Chubba's bench total. "Fourth-highest score" ranks every regular-season and playoff score in this league on Sleeper, 2022 to now. Defense points use this league's scoring settings.

Chat quotes are from the league chat, lightly trimmed; a real name inside a quote is replaced with the manager's nickname in brackets. Transactions are from the league's own log. This league uses rolling waivers, not FAAB, so there are no bids to report.
