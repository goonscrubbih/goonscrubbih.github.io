The Promise vs the Curve

Stay Questionable and Pukachu are the highest-projected game of the week, 274 points combined. Deion promised the playoffs in September and is 4–1. Goudariddance has the worst luck in league history and leads their series 4–2.

Oct 15, 2026Stay Questionable (4–1) vs I Choose You Pukachu! (2–3)Week 6 preview

Neither of these managers has been the Matchup of the Week before. Deion has never been featured at all. That's strange, because Deion is the league's instigator. By the end of September he had tagged Jarbelo 10 times, Zeus 10 and ThaBrad 8. Goudariddance is the opposite. He has posted eight messages in this chat, ever.

One of them talks. The other one keeps losing close games. On Sunday the man with the promise plays the man with the curve.

Goudariddance leads 4–2, and the shape of it is the whole story of both men. Goudariddance wins big when he wins. Deion's two wins came by 7 and 11. The last meeting, Week 9 last season, went to Goudariddance by 4.62.

Look at their careers side by side and you'd expect the series to be lopsided the other way. Deion came into this season 27–29 with a .468 all-play. That's an honest record for an average team. Goudariddance came in 26–30 on a .568 all-play, the worst luck anyone in this league has had. He was third in points in 2022 and went 6–8. He was third again last year and went 8–6.

Last year the points finally turned into a run. Goudariddance beat Buttons in the first round, lost to Tbunzz, the eventual champion, and then beat Jarbelo in the third-place game. Jarbelo had gone 12–2 that year and finished fourth. Deion has made the playoffs once, in 2023, lost to JBV in the first round and won the fifth-place game.

Stay Questionable: the promise

On Sept. 8, Deion told the chat: "We making playoffs this year." Five weeks later he is 4–1, fourth in points and 36–19 against the field. Of all the preseason promises in this chat, his is the one holding.

He has done it after admitting he misread the draft. On Oct. 1 he explained that he went in "thinking they're all noobs I'll snag a TE late and they all started taking 2 in like the 6th round." His answer was to hoard. Stay Questionable now rosters three tight ends and starts two of them, Mark Andrews and Brenton Strange, with Darren Waller on the bench. It's 20 projected points from the position he planned to ignore.

Joe Burrow is on bye, so Deion starts Tyler Shough, the Saints' quarterback, against the Giants. Shough has averaged 18.0. Bijan Robinson is projected 26.3, the most of anyone in this game. Then there's Mike Evans. In September, Goon tried to pry him loose: "I miss the hardness, wanna trade?" Deion kept him. Last week Evans scored 26.3 against Goon. This week he plays Monday night in San Francisco, which gives Deion the last word.

Deion also spent Saturday predicting where Marvin Harrison Jr. would be traded. "Texans would be pretty nasty with him and Nico." Then, 49 minutes later: "Yup def won't be Texans lol." He went to the Texans on Sunday.

Pukachu: the curve

Goudariddance named his team after Puka Nacua, and Puka has delivered: 37.7 last week, 25.9 a game this season, projected 22.4 on Sunday against Arizona. Trey McBride adds 16.5. Pukachu starts two tight ends too, McBride and Travis Kelce. Deion hoarded tight ends; Goudariddance drafted the good ones.

The problem has been the running backs, and the choices. In Week 4 he started David Montgomery over Brian Robinson, and Robinson outscored him by 21 on the bench. He lost by 0.08. In Week 5 he started Montgomery again, and Montgomery scored 1.1. This week Montgomery is benched and the backfield is RJ Harvey and Ashton Jeanty, projected 24 combined. Bijan alone is projected 26.

He also dropped Alvin Kamara on Sept. 8, before Kamara's three-team tour of the league. And he is sitting Lamar Jackson, projected 20.9, behind Matthew Stafford at 21.7. That's the right call by a point. It's also a lot of quarterback on the bench while Deion starts Shough.

RJ Harvey plays tonight in Denver. If Harvey has a big Thursday, Pukachu starts Sunday with the lead. Deion has no one playing tonight.

The call

As set at 11:02 Thursday, it's 141.55 to 132.09. Deion has the better projection, the better record and the hotter all-play. Goudariddance has the series, the best player in the game in Puka, and a Thursday head start.

The desk picks Stay Questionable. Bijan against a running-back room that can't match him is the gap, and Evans gets Monday night. But this is the manager whose luck never evens out, playing the man he's beaten four times out of six. If the curve ever bends back toward Goudariddance, it bends in a week like this one. Last week the desk picked right; it was wrong the week before. Deion knows how these promises go.

How this was put together

Projections are Sleeper's Week 6 PPR projections summed over each team's starting lineup as set at 11:02 a.m. ET Thursday, Oct 15. Injury tags are Sleeper's at the same time.
